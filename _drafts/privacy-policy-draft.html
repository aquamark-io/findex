1. Who we are

FinDex is an evidence-based reporting database for the FinTech industry. It is operated by Duncan Consulting LLC, doing business as FinDex (“FinDex,” “we,” “us”), based in San Francisco, California.

This policy covers our website at joinfindex.com and the FinDex portal at app.joinfindex.com. It explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights you have.

2. FinDex and the Fair Credit Reporting Act

FinDex collects reports about individuals, partner companies, and funded businesses, and makes published reports available to verified organizations with a permissible purpose. We operate FinDex in accordance with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA).

In practice, this means:

  • Only verified organizations can search, and every search must state a permissible purpose: employment screening, partner diligence, or underwriting. Employment screening also requires the employer’s certification and the individual’s signed authorization.
  • Every report must be supported by evidence and is independently reviewed before anything is published.
  • Reported parties are notified by email, with the reporting company named, at least 10 days before a report is published. Individuals also receive the CFPB’s A Summary of Your Rights Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act.
  • Anyone can view and dispute what has been reported about them, at no cost.

3. Information we collect

Member organizations

When a company requests access, we collect the requester’s name, business email address, job title, and phone number, and the company’s legal name, DBA, website, company type, and state of registration. We also record acceptance of the Member Terms and acknowledgment of the CFPB Notice to Users and Notice to Furnishers.

People and businesses checking their own record

  • Individuals: name, email address, date of birth, the last four digits of their Social Security number, current or most recent company, job title, and type of work.
  • Companies and funded businesses: the contact’s name and email address, and the business’s legal name, DBA, website, and EIN (or, for a business without an EIN, the owner’s date of birth and the last four digits of their Social Security number).

We use these details to verify identity and to match a person or business to reports about them.

Reports and evidence

When a member submits a report, we collect information about the reported party (such as name, email address, contact person, website, date of birth, last four digits of a Social Security number, or EIN), a description of the incident, its date, and supporting evidence files. Reporters must redact full Social Security and bank account numbers from evidence before submitting it.

Disputes

When a reported party disputes a report, we collect their explanation and any files they provide, and the reporting company’s response and files.

Searches

When a member searches, we record the identifiers entered, the stated permissible purpose, the certification, any employment authorization form, who searched and when, and which reports were shown.

Technical information

We record IP addresses and timestamps for account and report activity, email delivery events (such as bounced notices), and the cookies described in section 7.

4. How we use information

  • To verify organizations and individuals and operate accounts, including passwordless sign-in.
  • To review reports and evidence, notify reported parties, publish verified reports, and handle disputes.
  • To return search results to members with a permissible purpose, and to keep the records the FCRA requires, including who accessed a record and why.
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse, and investigate falsified reports.
  • To send service emails, such as sign-in codes, notices, and dispute updates.
  • To comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. How information is shared

  • With members who search: published reports only, showing the incident date, category, reporting company, description, and evidence. Report details never show dates of birth, Social Security numbers, or EINs, and reporters must redact full Social Security and bank account numbers from evidence.
  • With reported parties: the notice names the reporting company and describes the report. Reported parties can view the report and its evidence, and can see which organizations accessed their record.
  • With reporting companies: the reported party’s dispute, explanation, and files about that company’s report, so it can respond.
  • With service providers who host and operate FinDex for us, under contracts that limit their use of the information.
  • When required by law, such as a subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights and safety of FinDex, our members, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

6. AI-assisted evidence review

Our reviewers may use an AI tool to summarize evidence against what each report category requires. Evidence files and the report details are sent to our AI provider for this purpose. The report details include only the last four digits of an SSN or EIN. The summary is an aid for our reviewers: every decision to publish, reject, or remove a report is made by a person.

7. Cookies

The portal uses only cookies that are strictly necessary to sign you in and keep you signed in: a session cookie that lasts up to 12 hours, and a short-lived cookie used during sign-in. These cookies are not readable by scripts on the page. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and our website loads no third-party tracking scripts.

8. How long we keep information

  • Reports and evidence are permanently deleted 7 years after the incident date, unless the report is in an open dispute at that time.
  • Removed and rejected reports are kept on the same schedule as a record of what was submitted and decided.
  • Search results can be viewed by the searching member for 7 days. Search and access logs, and employment authorization forms, are kept as long as required by law.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and can be used once.
  • Account information is kept while the account is active and afterwards as long as needed to meet legal obligations.

9. How we protect information

  • Encryption: all connections use HTTPS. Dates of birth, the last four digits of Social Security numbers, and EINs are encrypted in our database with AES-256-GCM, and are masked wherever they appear.
  • Never in emails or links: these identifiers are never included in any email, log, or web address.
  • Restricted database access: the database cannot be reached directly from the internet or by any public key. Only the FinDex server can read or write it.
  • Private evidence storage: evidence files are stored privately. Each time an authorized person opens one, they get a link that expires after 2 minutes, and the access is logged.
  • Passwordless sign-in: members sign in with a one-time code sent to their email, so there are no passwords to steal or reuse. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited.
  • Audit trail: report decisions, evidence access, disputes, and searches are logged.
  • Minimal sharing: report details sent to our AI provider include only the last four digits of an SSN or EIN, and reporters must redact full Social Security and bank account numbers from evidence.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

10. Your rights and choices

  • See your record: create a free account with “Check My Record” to see every report about you or your business, and the organizations that accessed it.
  • Dispute: dispute any report at no cost. Disputed reports are withheld while we reinvestigate, and we complete our review within 30 days. Inaccurate or unverifiable information is corrected or removed.
  • Update or delete your account: contact us. Some information may be kept where the law requires it, such as records of reports, disputes, and searches.

11. Children

FinDex is a business service and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will notify members and account holders by email before it takes effect.

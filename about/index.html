About FinDex

FinDex is an independently administered reporting database for commercial finance. Verified companies can search for and report individual misconduct and business funding defaults. Every report requires supporting evidence. Individuals and businesses can view and dispute reports about them for free.

Who can search and report

Verified brokerages, lenders, and service providers can request access. Each company can have one master account. Searches for individuals are subject to FCRA and can only be used for employment screening.

What can be reported

FinDex accepts reports involving individuals, business partners, and funded businesses. Examples include data theft or backdooring, document manipulation, impersonation, misleading statements, and defaults on business funding.

Every report requires supporting evidence and is reviewed by a person before publication. Attestations alone are not accepted.

Your rights

Anyone reported is notified and has 10 days to respond before publication. Disputed reports are withheld during review, and unverifiable information is corrected or removed. Identifiers are encrypted, and records are deleted after 7 years.

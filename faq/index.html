Frequently Asked Questions

Why is FinDex free?

FinDex is the first evidence-based reporting database for commercial finance. We’re keeping it free so companies across the industry can help build a reliable record from the start. As the database grows, so will the cost of maintaining it. If we introduce pricing, we’ll let you know well in advance and keep affordability and accessibility at the center of that decision.

Who can search and report?

Only verified companies can search and submit reports. Each company can have one account. Before we approve your account, we confirm that your business registration is active, your website is live, and your work email uses the same domain as your website.

What do I see when I search?

Search results show published reports only.

  • Individuals and vendors: The date, description, and supporting evidence for each reported incident.
  • Funded businesses: The number and dates of reported defaults and document tampering incidents, plus how many times other companies searched for the business in the past 30 days.
Can I browse a list of reports?

No. To protect privacy and prevent solicitation, FinDex doesn’t display lists of reported businesses or individuals. You only see results for the specific business or person you search for.

Is there an API?

Yes. Request API access from your dashboard. See the API documentation.

Can I see and dispute what’s been reported about me?

Yes. Select “Check My Record” to create a free account. We confirm your details before approving it, then you can view and dispute reports about you or your business at no cost. Disputed reports are withheld while we review the evidence, and inaccurate or unverifiable information is corrected or removed.

Will I be told if I’m reported?

Yes. We’ll email you, tell you which company submitted the report, and give you 10 days to respond before it can be published.

What is a vendor?

A vendor is a company you work with, such as:

  • CRM or LOS
  • Underwriting & Decisioning
  • Document Processing & Fraud Detection
  • Lead & Data Providers
  • Marketplaces & Submission Platforms
  • Payments, Servicing & Collections
  • Outsourced Operations
  • IT & Security
  • Legal & Compliance
  • Broker partner
  • Lender partner
  • Other Service Providers
What can be reported?
  • Individuals: Data theft, backdooring, document tampering, impersonation, and misleading statements to merchants.
  • Vendors: Data theft, backdooring, mishandling of your data, impersonation, and misleading statements.
  • Funded businesses: Document tampering and business funding defaults. Only lenders can report defaults.
What evidence does FinDex accept?

Examples include, but are not limited to:

  • Data theft (backdooring) — Emails, call recordings, screenshots, access logs, or file transfer records.
  • Document tampering — The original and suspected altered versions, with an explanation of what changed, or results from your fraud detection provider.
  • Impersonation or misleading statements — Emails, call recordings, chat messages, or screenshots.
  • Mishandling of data — Emails, call recordings, or access logs that show how the data was handled.
  • Business funding defaults — A copy of the default notice sent to the business.
What happens if a company submits a false report?

We remove reports found to contain false information. Companies that repeatedly submit falsified reports may lose access and may themselves be reported.

How long are records kept?

We permanently delete records and supporting evidence after seven years unless a dispute is still open. Reports of misconduct can be submitted up to seven years after the incident. Business funding defaults must be reported within 90 days.

How is my information protected?

FinDex never asks for a Social Security number. We encrypt and mask dates of birth and EINs and keep them out of search results and emails. We store supporting evidence on infrastructure covered by a SOC 2 Type II audit.

How does FinDex comply with the FCRA?

Individual searches are limited to employment screening. Every search is logged and requires signed background check authorization. Before a report is published, the individual receives notice and the CFPB’s Summary of Rights. They can see who accessed their record, and FinDex reinvestigates disputes within 30 days.

Vendor and funded-business reports fall outside the FCRA, but still follow the same evidence, notice, and dispute standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is FinDex free?

FinDex is the first evidence-based reporting database built for commercial finance, and a reliable record has to be built from the start. Free access invites verified organizations to contribute verifiable incidents, not attestations. Checking and disputing your own record will always be free. As the database grows, so will the cost of maintaining it. Any future pricing will be announced well in advance, with affordability and accessibility remaining priorities.

Who can search FinDex?

Verified commercial finance companies: brokers, lenders, software providers, and service providers. One account per company. Searches of individuals are subject to the FCRA and offered for employment screening only. Searches of partners and funded businesses are not subject to the FCRA.

Can I browse a list of reported merchants or companies?

No. Searches are exact matches only, such as a funded business’s EIN. FinDex does not provide lists, to protect privacy and prevent solicitation.

Is there an API?

Yes, on request, for default searches of funded businesses. Search by EIN, or by legal name and state, to check whether a business has a default on record in FinDex.

Can I see and dispute what’s been reported about me?

Yes. Select “Check My Record” to create a free account and verify your identity. You can view and dispute reports about you or your business at no cost. Disputed reports are withheld while we review the evidence, and inaccurate or unverifiable information is corrected or removed.

Will I be told if I’m reported?

Yes. You’ll receive an email naming the company that submitted the report, and you’ll have 10 days to respond before it’s published.

What can be reported?
  • Individuals: data theft or backdooring, document manipulation, and misleading statements to merchants.
  • Partners: data theft or backdooring, mishandling of your data, and misleading statements.
  • Funded businesses: document manipulation and defaults. Only lenders may report defaults.

Reports must be supported by documented, verifiable evidence, such as emails, file transfer logs, call recordings, or default notices. Attestations alone are not accepted, and every report is independently reviewed before publication.

What happens if a company submits a false report?

Reports found to contain false information are removed. Companies that repeatedly submit falsified reports may lose access and be reported themselves.

How long are records kept?

Incidents can be reported up to 7 years after they occurred. Records and evidence are permanently deleted after 7 years, unless in dispute.

Is my information secure?

FinDex never asks for a Social Security number. Date of birth and EIN are encrypted, masked everywhere they appear, and never shown to searchers or included in emails. Evidence is kept on SOC 2 Type II infrastructure. Every publication decision is made by a person.

How does FinDex comply with the FCRA?

Individual searches are offered for employment screening only. Every search is logged and requires the individual’s signed authorization. Reported individuals are notified with the CFPB Summary of Rights before publication, disputes are reinvestigated within 30 days, and you can see who accessed your record. Partner and funded-business reports are not covered by the FCRA but follow the same evidence, notice, and dispute standards.

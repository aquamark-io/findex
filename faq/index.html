Frequently Asked Questions

Why is FinDex free?

FinDex is free so companies across commercial finance can help build a reliable record from the start. Free doesn’t mean unvetted: only verified companies can search or submit reports, and every report is reviewed for supporting evidence before it’s published. If fees are ever introduced, they’ll be announced well in advance, with affordability and accessibility as priorities.

Who can search and report others?

Verified companies only. One account per company.

Who can be reported?
  • Employees
  • Contractors
  • Vendors
  • Merchants
What can be reported?
  • Accidental Data Exposure
  • Data Theft (Backdooring)
  • Defaulted on Financing
  • Document Tampering
  • False or Misleading Statements
  • Impersonation
  • Operating without State License
  • Operating without State Registration
  • Unauthorized Fees
  • Violation of FCRA, ECOA, UDAP, or TCPA
  • Other (Contact Us)
What is a vendor?

A vendor is a company you work with, such as:

  • CRM or LOS
  • Underwriting & Decisioning
  • Document Processing & Fraud Detection
  • Lead & Data Providers
  • Marketplaces & Submission Platforms
  • Payments, Servicing & Collections
  • Outsourced Operations
  • IT & Security
  • Legal & Compliance
  • Broker partner
  • Lender partner
  • Other Service Providers
What is a merchant?

A merchant is a business that applied for or received funding.

What evidence does FinDex accept?

Please redact personally identifiable information that isn’t needed to verify the incident before submitting evidence. Examples include the following.

  • Emails
  • Text messages
  • Online chat transcripts
  • Call recordings
  • Screenshots
  • Screen recordings
  • System access and activity logs
  • Applications, bank statements, and other supporting documents
  • Fraud detection reports
  • Invoices, payment requests, and receipts
  • Funding and commission records
  • Default notices and related correspondence
  • Data breach or incident notifications
  • Written policies
  • Fee schedules
What do I see when I search?

Depending on the incident type:

  • Date
  • Reporting Party
  • Reporting Category
  • Description
  • Evidence
  • Number of searches last 30 days
Can I browse a list of reports?

No. To protect privacy and prevent solicitation, FinDex doesn’t display lists of reported businesses or individuals.

How does FinDex comply with the FCRA?

Only verified companies can search for individuals, and only for employment screening. FinDex requires the individual’s written authorization, logs every search, and notifies the individual, with the CFPB Summary of Rights, before any report about them is published. Individuals can access their record for free, see who requested it, and dispute inaccuracies. FinDex generally completes reinvestigations within 30 days of receiving a dispute.

Can I see and dispute what’s been reported about me?

Yes. Select “Check My Record” to create a free account and dispute reports about you or your business at no cost.

Will I be told if I’m reported?

Yes. We’ll email you, tell you which company submitted the report, and give you 10 days to respond before it can be published.

What happens if a company submits a false report?

We remove reports found to contain false information. Companies that repeatedly submit falsified reports may lose access and may themselves be reported.

How long are records kept?

We permanently delete records and supporting evidence after seven years unless a dispute is still open.

How is my information protected?

FinDex never asks for a Social Security number. We encrypt and mask dates of birth and EINs and keep them out of search results and emails. We store supporting evidence on infrastructure covered by a SOC 2 Type II audit.

Is there an API?

Yes. Request API access from your dashboard. See the API documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is FinDex free?

FinDex is the first evidence-based reporting database built for commercial finance, and a reliable record has to be built from the start. Free access invites verified organizations to contribute verifiable incidents, not attestations. Checking and disputing your own record will always be free. As the database grows, so will the cost of maintaining it. Any future pricing will be announced well in advance, with affordability and accessibility remaining priorities.

Who can search and report?

Verified brokerages, lenders, software providers, and service providers can request access. Each company can have one master account. Searches for individuals are subject to FCRA and can only be used for employment screening. Searches for business partners and funded businesses are not subject to FCRA.

What do I see when I search?

Search results show published reports only. What you see depends on who you search for.

  • Funded businesses: The number and dates of reported defaults and document tampering incidents, plus how many times other companies searched for the business in the past 30 days.
  • Individuals and business partners: The date, description, and supporting evidence for each reported incident.
Can I browse a list of reports?

To protect privacy and prevent solicitation, FinDex doesn’t display lists of reported businesses or individuals, or the results of your search.

Is there an API?

Yes. Request API access from your dashboard. See the API documentation.

Can I see and dispute what’s been reported about me?

Yes. Select “Check My Record” to create a free account and verify your identity. You can view and dispute reports about you or your business at no cost. Disputed reports are withheld while we review the evidence, and inaccurate or unverifiable information is corrected or removed.

Will I be told if I’m reported?

Yes. We’ll email you, tell you which company submitted the report, and give you 10 days to respond before it can be published.

What can be reported?
  • Individuals: Data theft, backdooring, document tampering, impersonation, and misleading statements to merchants.
  • Business partners: Data theft, backdooring, mishandling of your data, impersonation, and misleading statements.
  • Funded businesses: Document tampering and business funding defaults. Only lenders can report defaults.
What happens if a company submits a false report?

We remove reports found to contain false information. Companies that repeatedly submit falsified reports may lose access and may themselves be reported.

How long are records kept?

We permanently delete records and supporting evidence after seven years unless a dispute is still open. Reports of misconduct can be submitted up to seven years after the incident. Business funding defaults must be reported within 90 days.

How is my information protected?

FinDex never asks for a Social Security number. We encrypt and mask dates of birth and EINs and keep them out of search results and emails. We store supporting evidence on infrastructure covered by a SOC 2 Type II audit.

How does FinDex comply with the FCRA?

Individual searches are offered for employment screening only. Every search is logged and requires the individual’s signed authorization. Reported individuals are notified with the CFPB Summary of Rights before publication, disputes are reinvestigated within 30 days, and you can see who accessed your record. Partner and funded-business reports are not covered by the FCRA but follow the same evidence, notice, and dispute standards.

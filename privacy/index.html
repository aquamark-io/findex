Privacy Policy

Version 1.01

1. Who we are

FinDex is an independently administered, FCRA-compliant reporting database for commercial finance. It is operated by Duncan Consulting LLC, doing business as FinDex (“FinDex,” “we,” “us”), based in San Francisco, California.

This policy covers our website at joinfindex.com, the FinDex portal at app.joinfindex.com, and the FinDex Merchant API. It explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and your rights and choices.

2. FinDex and the Fair Credit Reporting Act

FinDex collects evidence-based reports about individuals, vendors, and merchants, and makes published reports available to verified companies with a permissible purpose. We operate FinDex in accordance with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). In practice:

  • Only verified companies can search, and every search must state a permissible purpose: employment screening (individuals), partner due diligence (vendors), or underwriting (merchants). Employment screening also requires the employer’s certification that the individual signed a background check authorization.
  • Every report must be supported by evidence and is reviewed before anything is published.
  • Reported parties are emailed a notice, naming the reporting company, at least 10 days before a report is published. Individuals also receive the CFPB’s A Summary of Your Rights Under the Fair Credit Reporting Act.
  • Anyone can view and dispute what has been reported about them, at no cost.

3. Information we collect

Members. When a company requests access, we collect the requester’s name, business email address, job title, and phone number, and the company’s legal name, DBA, website, company type, and state of registration. We also record acceptance of the Member Terms and acknowledgment of the CFPB Notice to Users and Notice to Furnishers.

People and businesses checking their own record.

  • Individuals: name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, most recent company, job title, and whether they are an employee or contractor.
  • Vendors: the contact’s name, business email address, phone number, and job title, and the company’s legal name, DBA, website, company type, and state of registration.
  • Merchants: the contact’s name, email address, phone number, and title, and the business’s legal name, DBA, state of registration, website (optional), and EIN.

We use these details to verify identity and to match a person or business to reports about them. FinDex never asks for any part of a Social Security number.

Reports and evidence. When a member submits a report, we collect information about the reported party, a description of the incident, its category and date, and supporting evidence files. Depending on who is reported, this may include:

  • Individuals: name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, and home ZIP code (optional).
  • Vendors: company name, website, vendor type, contact person, and contact email.
  • Merchants: legal name, DBA, EIN, state of registration, contact person, and contact email.

Before submitting evidence, reporters must remove personally identifiable information that isn’t needed to verify the incident, including full Social Security and bank account numbers.

Disputes. When a reported party disputes a report, we collect their explanation and any files they provide, and the reporting company’s response and files.

Searches. When a member searches in the portal or through the Merchant API, we record who searched and when, the stated purpose and certification, any employment authorization form, the name, email, and website entered, and which reports were shown. We record whether a date of birth, EIN, phone number, or ZIP code was used, but not the values themselves. For business searches, we keep a one-way key so we can count recent searches of the same business.

Contact messages. When you use our contact form, we collect your name, email address, topic, message, and IP address.

Technical information. We record IP addresses and timestamps for sign-ins and account, report, and admin activity, email delivery events (such as bounced notices), and the cookies described in section 7.

4. How we use information

  • To verify companies and people and operate accounts, including sign-in with one-time codes.
  • To review reports and evidence, notify reported parties, publish verified reports, and handle disputes.
  • To return search results to members with a permissible purpose, and to keep the records the FCRA requires, including who accessed a record and why.
  • To answer contact messages.
  • To secure FinDex, prevent abuse, and investigate falsified reports.
  • To send service emails, such as sign-in codes, notices, and dispute updates.
  • To comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. How information is shared

  • With members who search: published reports only, showing the incident date, category, reporting company, description, and evidence. Search results show which details matched, but never show dates of birth, EINs, or phone numbers. Merchant search results also show how many other companies searched the same business in the last 30 days, without naming them.
  • With reported parties: the notice names the reporting company and describes the report. Reported parties can view the report and its evidence, and see which companies accessed their record.
  • With reporting companies: the reported party’s dispute, explanation, and files about that company’s report, so it can respond.
  • With service providers who host and operate FinDex for us (application hosting, database and file storage, email delivery, and AI-assisted evidence review), under contracts that limit their use of the information.
  • When required by law, such as a subpoena or court order, or to protect the rights and safety of FinDex, our members, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

6. AI-assisted evidence review

Our reviewers may use an AI tool to check evidence against FinDex’s evidence requirements. Evidence files and report details are sent to our AI provider for this purpose. Report details include only the last four digits of an EIN, and never a date of birth. The summary helps our reviewers: every decision to publish, reject, or remove a report is made by a person.

7. Cookies

The portal uses only cookies that are strictly necessary to sign you in and keep you signed in: a session cookie that lasts up to 12 hours, and a cookie that lasts 15 minutes during sign-in. These cookies can’t be read by scripts on the page. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and our website loads no third-party tracking scripts.

8. How long we keep information

  • Reports and evidence are permanently deleted 7 years after the incident date, unless the report is in an open dispute at that time.
  • Removed and rejected reports are kept on the same schedule, as a record of what was submitted and decided.
  • Search results can be viewed by the searching member for 7 days. Search and access records, and employment authorization forms, are kept as long as the law requires.
  • Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes and can be used once.
  • Account information and contact messages are kept while the account is active, and afterwards as long as needed to meet legal obligations.

9. How we protect information

  • Encryption: all connections use HTTPS. Dates of birth and EINs are encrypted in our database with AES-256-GCM and masked wherever they appear.
  • Never in emails or links: dates of birth and EINs are never included in any email, log, or web address.
  • Restricted database access: the database can’t be reached directly from the internet or with any public key. Only the FinDex server can read or write it.
  • Private evidence storage: evidence files are stored privately on SOC 2 Type II infrastructure. Each time an authorized person opens one, they get a link that expires after 2 minutes, and the access is logged.
  • Sign-in codes, no passwords: members and FinDex staff sign in with a one-time code sent to their email, so there are no passwords to steal or reuse. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited.
  • Audit trail: report decisions, evidence access, disputes, and searches are logged. Every action by FinDex staff is recorded in an audit log that can’t be edited or deleted.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident affecting your information, we will notify you as required by law.

10. Your rights and choices

  • See your record: create a free account with Check My Record to see every report about you or your business, and the companies that accessed it.
  • Dispute: dispute any report at no cost. Disputed reports are withheld while we reinvestigate, and we complete our review within 30 days. Inaccurate or unverifiable information is corrected or removed.
  • Update or delete your account: use our contact page. Some information may be kept where the law requires it, such as records of reports, disputes, and searches.

11. Children

FinDex is a business service and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will email members and account holders before it takes effect.

13. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy: please use our contact page.

How we protect your information

FinDex never asks for a Social Security number. Date of birth and EIN are encrypted, masked everywhere they appear, and never shown to searchers or included in emails. Evidence is kept on SOC 2 Type II infrastructure, and records are permanently deleted after 7 years.

The standard

Built to be trusted by everyone on the record.

01

Membership requires approval

Every broker and funder is verified before any search or submission: official business registration, a business email that matches the company website, and an authorized account owner. No personal email accounts.

02

Evidence, not accusations

A report needs documentation, not just a statement. Each allegation is linked to the records behind it, and FinDex reviews them before anything moves forward.

03

Notice and the right to respond

The person or business reported is notified and has 10 business days to respond, with or without documents. A reviewer decides. Silence never publishes a report.

04

Built around the FCRA

The employment reporting program is designed around the Fair Credit Reporting Act: permissible purpose, candidate authorization, accuracy, free file access, disputes and corrections.

05

Authorized search only

Members search published records for a stated, permitted purpose. Uncertain identity matches release nothing. No bulk lists, no scraping, no redistribution.

06

Free for the people on record

Responding, requesting your record and disputing information are free and need no account. Inaccurate or unverifiable information is corrected or removed.

No scores. No blacklists. No recommendations.

FinDex shows the facts, the dates, the evidence reviewed and whether a response was received. Members make their own hiring and funding decisions. FinDex is not a court and does not guarantee complete histories.

Evidence-based reporting for commercial finance.

Know the Truth Before You Hire or Fund.

FinDex is a reporting database for brokers and funders. Verified businesses submit documented reports, FinDex reviews the evidence, and the person or business reported has the opportunity to respond before anything is published.

How it works

  1. 01

    Submit evidence

    A verified member submits a specific, dated account with supporting records. Each statement is linked to the evidence behind it. An attestation alone is not enough.

  2. 02

    Review and response

    FinDex reviews the evidence. The individual or business is notified and given 10 business days to respond, with or without supporting documents. A reviewer decides. Silence never publishes a report.

  3. 03

    Authorized search

    Verified members search published records for a stated, permitted purpose. Results show the facts, dates, evidence reviewed and response status. FinDex does not score, rank or recommend.

Two programs

Individuals

Documented misconduct, for hiring decisions

Who reports and searches
Verified brokers and funders, for authorized hiring purposes.
Categories
Theft of money, property, company data, merchant data or other documented assets.
Misleading merchants through materially false or misleading statements.
Other matters: contact FinDex to discuss eligibility.
What it is not
Performance issues, personality disputes or moving to a competitor are not reportable.

Merchants

Contractual defaults, for funding decisions

Who reports
Verified funders.
Who searches
Verified brokers and funders, for commercial financing decisions.
Standard
The merchant's own executed agreement defines default. FinDex sets no separate threshold. Funders show the clause, the payment history and any notices, cure periods, reconciliations or modifications.
Status
Records are updated for cure, repayment, settlement and modification. A default is a contractual status, not a finding of fraud.

Evidence standards

Every report is reviewed against what the records show.

  • Specific facts, dates and source context, from someone with direct knowledge or authorized access to the records.
  • Records that tie the act to the individual, or the default to the merchant's contract.
  • Screenshots that show dates, the source system and enough context to assess the claim. Original exports preferred.
  • Unrelated personal and financial information redacted.
  • Reported statements, evidence, responses and reviewer findings kept separate and versioned.

Responses and disputes

Responding, requesting your record and disputing information are free and do not require an account or membership.

  • Notice before publication, with a clear deadline and time zone.
  • Access to the proposed facts and the evidence relied on, with third-party information protected.
  • A written response and optional documents. No one is required to produce records they no longer have.
  • Disputes remain open after publication, with corrections and removal where information is inaccurate, incomplete or cannot be verified.

Our approach to employment reporting

The individual program is being built with the Fair Credit Reporting Act in mind. Hiring employers keep their own obligations, including candidate disclosure, authorization and adverse action notices.

  • Access limited to verified businesses that certify an employment purpose.
  • Candidate authorization required for individual searches.
  • Evidence review before publication, and exact identity matching. Uncertain matches release nothing.
  • Free file access, responses, disputes, reinvestigation and corrections for individuals.

Founding access

Founding access is available at no cost while we build the FinDex database. Business verification is required. Any future pricing will be communicated in advance, with no automatic enrollment in a paid plan.

  • Open to brokers and funders, verified by business registration and a business email that matches the company website.
  • One workspace per company, with individual logins for each authorized user.
  • No credit card required.

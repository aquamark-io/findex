1. Who we are

FinDex ("FinDex," "we," "us," "our") operates a business-only reporting database for the commercial finance industry, including brokers, funders, lenders, software providers and lead providers. FinDex is operated by [Legal entity name], located in San Francisco, California.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how we protect it, and the rights available to people and businesses whose information appears in FinDex.

2. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our website.
  • Member organizations: approved companies with a FinDex account, and the authorized users who act on their behalf.
  • Reported parties: individuals (such as employees and contractors), companies, and merchants who are the subject of a report in FinDex.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Member organizations

To approve and maintain a business account, we collect:

  • Company name, business registration details, and company website
  • Business email address and contact details of the authorized account owner and users
  • Information needed to verify the business and the authorized users
  • Account activity, including searches performed and the stated purpose for each search
  • [If applicable] Billing information. Payments are processed by Stripe; FinDex does not store full card numbers.

FinDex is business-only. We do not offer personal accounts, and each company may hold one account.

3.2 Reports and evidence

When a member submits a report, we collect the report details and supporting documentation (for example, records, communications, or recordings). Reports must relate to a reportable category, such as theft, fraud, merchant deception, data mishandling, slow pay, or defaults, and fall within the past 7 years.

3.3 Reported parties

A report may include information about the reported individual, company, or merchant, such as name, business affiliation, and the facts and dates of the reported incident.

3.4 Disputes and requests

If you dispute a report, appeal, or request a copy of reports about you, we collect the information you provide, including your contact details and any supporting documents.

3.5 Website visitors

Our website is a static site. [Confirm: we do not use analytics or advertising cookies.] Fonts and other site assets are served from our own site; the site does not load third-party fonts or scripts. Our website host may log basic technical information, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Verify and approve member organizations and their authorized users
  • Operate the FinDex database, including accepting, reviewing, and publishing reports
  • Provide search results to members with a permissible purpose
  • Notify reported parties before publication and process disputes and appeals
  • Maintain the accuracy and integrity of records
  • Secure our systems, prevent misuse, and enforce our terms
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

5. Permissible purpose and FCRA

FinDex is built around responsible reporting practices and in accordance with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA).

  • Permissible purpose. Every search requires a stated, authorized purpose, such as underwriting, employment screening, or partner due diligence. Searches without a permissible purpose are not allowed.
  • Consent for employment searches. Searches on individuals for hiring purposes require the individual's signed consent.
  • Accuracy. Reports require supporting documentation and are independently reviewed before publication. Inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable information is not published.
  • Notice. Individuals and businesses are notified of reports about them before publication.

6. How evidence is handled

  • PII redaction. Personal information contained in evidence is redacted before the evidence is stored.
  • Secure infrastructure. Evidence is stored with service providers that maintain SOC 2 Type II reports. [Confirm which providers store evidence, e.g. Supabase.]
  • Retention and deletion. Evidence is permanently deleted after 7 years.

7. How we share information

We share information only as described here:

  • With members, when they search with a permissible purpose. Members see published records only.
  • With reported parties, who may request reports about themselves.
  • With service providers that help us operate FinDex under contract, such as hosting, database and storage, and payment processing. Current providers include [Supabase, Render, GitHub (website hosting), and Stripe (payments) — confirm list].
  • For legal reasons, when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of FinDex, our members, or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

Members may not redistribute, resell, scrape, or bulk-export FinDex records.

  • Dispute the accuracy or completeness of information
  • Appeal a decision

These requests are free, and you do not need an account. Disputed reports are unpublished while under review. Information found to be inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable is corrected or removed.
